---
title: "Loaded Breakfast Hash with Crispy Potatoes"
category: "Brunch"
prep_time: "15 min"
cook_time: "30 min"
serves: "4"
ingredients:
  - "1.5 lbs Yukon gold potatoes, diced small"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil, divided"
  - "1 red bell pepper, diced"
  - "1 small yellow onion, diced"
  - "8 oz breakfast sausage (check label — some use wheat-based fillers)"
  - "2 cups baby spinach"
  - "4 large eggs"
  - "Salt, pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder to taste"
  - "Hot sauce, for serving (check label for GF certification)"
instructions:
  - "Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large cast-iron or non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the potatoes in a single layer, season, and cook undisturbed for 4–5 minutes to let them crisp before stirring."
  - "Continue cooking, stirring occasionally, for 12–15 minutes until the potatoes are golden and fork-tender."
  - "Push the potatoes to one side. Add the remaining oil, then the sausage, breaking it up as it browns, about 5 minutes."
  - "Add the peppers and onion, cooking until softened, about 4 minutes. Stir everything together."
  - "Fold in the spinach until just wilted."
  - "Make four wells in the hash and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook 3–4 minutes until whites are set and yolks are still runny."
  - "Serve straight from the skillet with hot sauce."
featured: false
---
