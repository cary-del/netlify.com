---
title: "Flourless Chocolate Chip Blondies"
category: "Dessert"
prep_time: "10 min"
cook_time: "25 min"
serves: "9 squares"
ingredients:
  - "1 cup creamy almond butter (or sunflower seed butter for nut-free)"
  - "½ cup coconut sugar or brown sugar"
  - "1 large egg"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "½ tsp baking soda"
  - "¼ tsp salt"
  - "½ cup gluten-free chocolate chips, plus extra for topping"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 350°F. Line an 8x8-inch baking pan with parchment paper."
  - "In a bowl, whisk together the almond butter, sugar, egg, and vanilla until smooth."
  - "Stir in the baking soda and salt, then fold in the chocolate chips."
  - "Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan and sprinkle a few extra chocolate chips on top."
  - "Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the edges are golden and a toothpick comes out mostly clean."
  - "Cool completely in the pan before slicing into squares — they firm up as they cool."
featured: false
---
Made with almond butter instead of flour, so there's zero risk of a flour mix-up — these come together in one bowl in about 10 minutes.
