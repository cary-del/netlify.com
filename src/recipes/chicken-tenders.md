---
title: "Crispy Baked Gluten-Free Chicken Tenders"
category: "Family Dinner"
prep_time: "15 min"
cook_time: "20 min"
serves: "4"
ingredients:
  - "1.5 lbs chicken tenderloins"
  - "1 cup buttermilk"
  - "1 tsp hot sauce (optional)"
  - "1 cup certified gluten-free breadcrumbs or crushed GF cornflakes"
  - "½ cup gluten-free all-purpose flour"
  - "2 tsp paprika"
  - "1 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1 tsp onion powder"
  - "½ tsp cayenne (optional)"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Olive oil spray"
instructions:
  - "Marinate the chicken tenders in buttermilk and hot sauce for at least 30 minutes (or up to overnight in the fridge)."
  - "Preheat oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Set a wire rack on top if you have one — it keeps the coating crispy on all sides."
  - "In a shallow dish, mix the gluten-free breadcrumbs, flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper."
  - "Remove tenders from the buttermilk one at a time, letting the excess drip off, and press firmly into the breadcrumb mixture to coat."
  - "Place on the prepared baking sheet and spray both sides lightly with olive oil."
  - "Bake for 18–22 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 165°F."
  - "Serve with your favorite dipping sauce (check the label to confirm it's GF)."
featured: true
---
A crunchy, oven-baked tender that doesn't rely on a deep fryer (read: no cross-contamination worries from shared oil). Great for kids and adults alike.
