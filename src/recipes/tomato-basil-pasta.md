---
title: "One-Pot Creamy Tomato Basil Gluten-Free Pasta"
category: "Dinner"
prep_time: "10 min"
cook_time: "25 min"
serves: "4"
ingredients:
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes"
  - "2 ½ cups vegetable or chicken broth (certified gluten-free)"
  - "12 oz certified gluten-free pasta (rice, corn, or chickpea-based)"
  - "1 tsp dried oregano"
  - "½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)"
  - "½ cup heavy cream"
  - "1 cup fresh basil, torn, plus more for serving"
  - "½ cup grated parmesan, plus more for serving"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
instructions:
  - "Heat olive oil in a large, deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Stir in the crushed tomatoes, broth, dried pasta, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Bring to a boil."
  - "Reduce heat to a simmer, cover, and cook 10–14 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is tender. Check it a couple minutes early — gluten-free pasta can go from perfect to mushy fast."
  - "Stir in the cream and parmesan until the sauce is glossy and coats the pasta."
  - "Remove from heat, fold in the fresh basil, and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve immediately, topped with extra parmesan and basil."
featured: false
---
A 30-minute weeknight dinner that cooks the gluten-free pasta right in the sauce, so you never have to worry about a shared pasta pot at a restaurant.
